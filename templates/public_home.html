What is Room 120?

Room 120 is a tucked-away speakeasy built for people who hate crowds but love a good night. Hidden behind The Draft Room, it’s your spot for proper drinks, real conversation, and a guaranteed seat.

  • Members-only lounge with hosted entry
  • Signature cocktails and rotating rare pours
  • Priority access for game nights & arena events
  • Private bookings for groups and celebrations
